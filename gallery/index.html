In the News

PGMG in the Press

Philippine Post

January 9, 2026  ·  Page 13

PGMG: A Night of Fun, Food and Camaraderie

The Philippine Post covered PGMG's holiday party — a five-hour celebration of community spirit featuring a sumptuous buffet dinner, raffles, games, and karaoke. Members and guests gathered for an evening of kwentuhan, kainan, kantahan at katuwaan, capping off the year with warmth and laughter.

The group looked forward to next year's events and projects, including a planned fund-raiser for a scholarship program for Filipino journalism and mass communication students.
